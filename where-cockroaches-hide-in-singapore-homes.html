Where cockroaches hide in Singapore homes

Cockroaches do not need a large opening to settle in a Singapore home. They often use the same high-risk areas for shelter and movement: cabinet hinges, cracks, appliance gaps, skirting, entry points, drain perimeters, wall voids, and outdoor or drain areas. These places offer cover and access, which is why control starts with an inspection of high-risk areas rather than spraying visible insects only. Treatment is chosen by species, so identifying whether American, German, brown-banded or Oriental cockroaches are present helps determine the right method.

In Singapore homes, cockroaches are commonly associated with high-risk hiding and entry points. In kitchens, check cabinet hinges, cracks and appliance gaps. Look along skirting and around entry points, because these are places where movement and entry can occur. Drain perimeters are another area to check. Wall voids can provide hidden shelter, while outdoor and drain areas can be additional activity zones. Because these are high-risk areas, an inspection should cover them before treatment begins. This helps locate hiding points rather than treating only visible cockroaches.

Treatment follows the hiding and entry points found during inspection. Gel baiting can be placed in cabinet hinges, cracks and appliance gaps. Residual insecticide can be applied around skirting, entry points and drain perimeters. Insecticidal dust can be used in wall voids, and granular bait can be used for outdoor and drain areas. Mild to moderate infestations usually need 1 to 2 visits, while severe ones need 2 to 3 visits over 3 to 4 weeks. Re-entry is safe 2 hours after treatment, and treated surfaces should not be mopped for 48 hours. Why do cockroaches hide in cabinet hinges and appliance gaps?

Cabinet hinges, cracks and appliance gaps are high-risk hiding areas in Singapore homes. An inspection checks these points before treatment. Gel baiting may then be used in cabinet hinges, cracks and appliance gaps when the treatment method fits the species.
