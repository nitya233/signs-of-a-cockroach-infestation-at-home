Signs of a cockroach infestation at home

Cockroach problems at home are easier to manage when you notice early signs. Rather than waiting for a large sighting, check the places cockroaches commonly use for shelter and movement. In Singapore homes, these can include cabinet hinges, cracks, appliance gaps, skirting, entry points, drain perimeters and wall voids. Look for activity in these high-risk areas and note which species may be present, because American, German, brown-banded and Oriental cockroaches can require different treatment methods. Early observation helps an inspector choose the right approach and limit the spread.

At home, the practical signs to check are tied to where cockroaches hide and enter. Inspect cabinet hinges, cracks, appliance gaps, skirting, entry points, drain perimeters, wall voids, and outdoor or drain areas. If cockroaches are seen in these places, note the location and avoid disturbing the area before an inspection. The species matters. American, German, brown-banded and Oriental cockroaches may not respond to the same method, so a general spray without identification can miss the source. A useful first step is to record where activity appears and whether it recurs.

An inspection of high-risk areas helps confirm where hiding and entry points are concentrated. Treatment may then include gel baiting in cabinet hinges, cracks and appliance gaps; residual insecticide around skirting, entry points and drain perimeters; insecticidal dust in wall voids; or granular bait for outdoor and drain areas. Mild to moderate infestations usually need 1 to 2 visits, while severe ones need 2 to 3 visits over 3 to 4 weeks. A written report follows each visit. Common questions

What is the first thing to check if I suspect cockroaches?

Check high-risk hiding and entry areas such as cabinet hinges, cracks, appliance gaps, skirting, entry points, drain perimeters, wall voids, and outdoor or drain areas. Note where activity appears so an inspection can confirm the species and the right treatment method.

Why does cockroach species matter when treating an infestation?

American, German, brown-banded and Oriental cockroaches can require different treatment methods. The method is chosen by species after an inspection. Using one general approach without identifying the species may not address where the cockroaches are hiding and entering.

How many visits are usually needed for cockroach control?

Mild to moderate infestations usually need 1 to 2 visits. Severe infestations usually need 2 to 3 visits over 3 to 4 weeks. A written report follows each visit, and re-entry is safe 2 hours after treatment.
